## Overview

The Mini MP3 Speaker is a completed engineering project developed in 2018 to demonstrate electronic waste repurposing through a practical audio system. The prototype uses an old mobile phone box as its enclosure, a salvaged MP3 module as the audio source, a recycled amplifier board, two recovered woofer speakers, and a repurposed power adapter.

The project demonstrates how discarded materials and reusable electronics can be combined into a functional sound system while developing practical skills in circuit assembly, audio integration, and enclosure fabrication.

## Development Timeline

- **August 2018 — Component Collection:** Gathered reusable electronics and scrap enclosure materials.
- **August 2018 — Enclosure Preparation:** Prepared the enclosure and mounted the speakers.
- **August 2018 — Electronics Assembly:** Integrated the MP3 module, amplifier, speakers, and power supply.
- **August–September 2018 — Testing:** Tested audio playback and adjusted component placement.
- **September 2018 — Project Completion:** Completed the functional upcycled speaker prototype.
